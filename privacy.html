WebCloud Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

WebCloud respects the privacy of people who visit our website, communicate with us, or engage us for digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how WebCloud may collect, receive, use, store, protect, and disclose information in connection with our website and services.

This policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions and any project-specific agreements that may apply to a client relationship.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with WebCloud, we may receive information such as your name, business name, email address, telephone number, social media username, website address, project requirements, business information, and other information that you voluntarily provide.

We may also receive information contained in messages, enquiries, project briefs, quotations, feedback, correspondence, or other communications.

3. Information You Choose to Provide

When you contact WebCloud through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, email, forms, or another communication channel, you choose what information to provide.

You should only provide information that is reasonably necessary for the purpose of the communication.

Please do not send passwords, banking PINs, payment card security codes, authentication codes, or other highly sensitive credentials through ordinary communication channels.

4. Project Information

If you become a WebCloud client, we may receive and process information required to plan, design, build, deploy, maintain, or support your project.

This may include brand assets, photographs, written content, business information, product information, service descriptions, contact information, links, technical requirements, hosting information, and other project materials.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate with clients, prepare quotations, understand project requirements, provide services, manage projects, provide support, improve our website, and maintain business records.

Information may also be used to communicate important project-related updates, service information, or administrative matters.

6. Website Usage Information

When you visit a website, certain technical information may potentially be made available through hosting infrastructure, browsers, devices, or other technical systems.

Depending on the tools and services active on the website, this may include information such as device type, browser type, approximate location, referring page, pages visited, and general interaction data.

WebCloud will only use such information where reasonably necessary for website operation, security, analysis, improvement, or another legitimate purpose.

7. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Websites may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, remember preferences, analyse usage, or improve user experience.

The exact cookies or technologies used may change as WebCloud updates its website and infrastructure.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

WebCloud may rely on third-party platforms and infrastructure to operate, deploy, communicate, analyse, secure, or support digital services.

Examples may include hosting providers, deployment platforms, communication platforms, analytics tools, domain providers, payment providers, cloud services, APIs, and other technology providers.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Communication Platforms

WebCloud may use external communication platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, email, or other services to communicate with prospective and existing clients.

Information shared through those platforms may also be subject to the privacy policies and security practices of the respective platform providers.

10. Payments

Where payments are accepted through a third-party payment provider, payment information may be processed directly by that provider.

WebCloud does not intend to unnecessarily collect or retain complete payment-card information through ordinary communication channels.

Clients should use the designated payment method provided for a project and should never send payment credentials through unsecured messages.

11. Information Security

WebCloud takes reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission, digital storage system, website, communication platform, or electronic service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You acknowledge that information transmitted over the internet carries some level of risk.

12. Confidential Project Information

WebCloud understands that clients may provide information about their businesses, products, operations, plans, customers, or projects that they consider confidential.

We will make reasonable efforts to handle such information responsibly and will not intentionally disclose confidential project information except where necessary to provide the service, required by law, or authorised by the client.

13. Portfolio & Publicity

WebCloud may wish to display completed projects as examples of its work.

If a client has confidentiality requirements or does not want a project publicly displayed, the client should communicate this requirement before the project is published or launched.

14. Data Retention

WebCloud may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate business, contractual, legal, security, accounting, or administrative purposes.

The period for which information is retained may vary depending on its nature and why it was collected.

15. Information Sharing

WebCloud does not intend to sell personal information simply because someone visits the website or contacts us.

Information may be shared with service providers, contractors, technology providers, or other parties where reasonably necessary to provide a requested service or operate the business.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, court order, regulatory authority, fraud prevention requirements, or protection of legal rights.

16. Children's Privacy

WebCloud's services are intended primarily for businesses, professionals, creators, organisations, and other users capable of entering into appropriate agreements.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing legitimate services.

17. External Websites

Our website may contain links to websites operated by other organisations, businesses, platforms, or service providers.

Once you leave the WebCloud website, your interaction is governed by the policies of the destination website.

WebCloud is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or availability of external websites.

18. Your Choices

You may choose whether to provide information when contacting WebCloud.

However, some information may be necessary to provide a quotation, communicate about a project, process a request, or deliver an agreed service.

19. Requests Concerning Your Information

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information held about you.

These rights may include requesting access, correction, deletion, restriction, or clarification concerning certain information.

Requests should be made through an official WebCloud communication channel and should contain enough information for us to understand and respond to the request.

20. Accuracy of Information

WebCloud relies on clients and users to provide accurate information where information is supplied directly to us.

If your information changes during a project, you should provide updated information where it is necessary for the project or service.

21. International Processing

Because websites and digital services may rely on globally distributed technology infrastructure, information may potentially be processed or stored in locations outside the country where you live.

Where third-party providers are involved, their own policies may describe the locations and mechanisms through which information is processed.

22. Data Breach & Security Incidents

If WebCloud becomes aware of a significant security incident affecting information under its control, reasonable steps may be taken to investigate, contain, and respond to the incident.

Where notification is required by applicable law, appropriate notifications may be made.

23. Changes to This Privacy Policy

WebCloud may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, services, business practices, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

The date displayed at the beginning of this policy indicates when it was most recently updated.

24. Relationship With the Terms & Conditions

This Privacy Policy explains privacy practices and should be read together with the WebCloud Terms & Conditions.

The Terms & Conditions establish the broader rules governing the use of the website and engagement of WebCloud services.

25. Contact WebCloud

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to make a privacy-related request, or need clarification concerning the handling of information, you can contact WebCloud through an official channel.

WhatsApp WebCloud Instagram Facebook

26. Final Privacy Statement

WebCloud believes that a professional digital relationship should be built on transparency, responsible communication, and respect for the information entrusted to us.

We will continue to review our practices as WebCloud grows and as technology and privacy expectations evolve.
